# Two-Minute Postra Demo Script

**Recording setup:** Open the live app at https://postra-pink.vercel.app/ before recording. Use a fictional demo business and non-sensitive details. Keep the browser zoom readable and close unrelated tabs or notifications.

## Timed walkthrough

**0:00–0:15 — Introduce the user and problem**  
“This is Postra, an AI marketing sidekick for Philippine small businesses without a marketing team. It helps an owner create a social post without having to write a complicated AI prompt.”

**0:15–0:35 — Show the guided form**  
“The owner describes the business, what they are promoting, and who the post is for. They can also select a platform, goal, tone, and language.”

**0:35–0:55 — Enter a fictional example**  
Use a fictional business such as **Demo Café**. Enter a specific offer, for example: “Freshly baked ube pandesal; buy two and get one free through Friday.” Set the audience to nearby commuters and families, choose Instagram, select a promotional goal, and choose a tone and language.

**0:55–1:20 — Generate the post**  
Click **Generate Caption** and wait for the result. “Postra returns a caption, five hashtags, and a creative suggestion for the post.” Briefly point to each part. Mention that the owner should review the result for accuracy before posting.

**1:20–1:40 — Show copy or regenerate**  
Click **Copy content** to demonstrate the copy action. If time permits, click **Regenerate** to show that Postra can create another version from the same brief.

**1:40–1:55 — Explain the product boundary**  
“Postra creates a draft for the owner. It does not publish automatically, save post history, or create an account.”

**1:55–2:00 — Close**  
“That is Postra: a guided way to get a social post draft and a creative starting point. The live app is at postra-pink.vercel.app.”

## Recording checklist

- Keep the finished recording close to two minutes.
- Show the real live app and one successful generation.
- Do not show API keys, Vercel settings, personal information, or private browser tabs.
- Upload the video somewhere accessible and test its share link before adding that link to the submission.
