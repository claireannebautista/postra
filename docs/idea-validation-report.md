# Postra Idea Validation Report

**Product:** Postra, an AI-powered social media post assistant  
**Primary user:** Philippine-based small-business owners who manage their own social media without a marketing team

## 1. Is the problem real?

I interviewed three Philippine-based businesses: a local bag manufacturer, a fruit and vegetable juice manufacturer, and a catering service. I found the participants through friends and work contacts and will keep their feedback anonymous. Each uses Instagram; two also use Facebook. Posting frequency ranged from about twice a month to three times a week.

Participants described uncertainty about whether a caption is engaging, difficulty promoting products without sounding too sales-focused, and reliance on ChatGPT for ideas or grammar. Their current approaches include writing captions themselves, using ChatGPT, and searching Pinterest for inspiration. This is early evidence that creating social posts can take effort for some small-business owners; it does not establish how common the problem is across all MSMEs.

## 2. Who is the target user?

Postra is for Philippine-based small-business owners who handle their own Facebook or Instagram marketing and do not have a marketing team. They need a quick way to create relevant social posts and may not have marketing training or confidence writing AI prompts. The interviews covered three different business types, so the primary user description should be tested with a larger and more varied group.

## 3. Is the market meaningful?

The Department of Trade and Industry reports 1,236,908 MSMEs in the Philippines in 2024, representing 99.63% of 1,241,476 registered business establishments. This indicates a broad potential business base. It is not Postra's total addressable market: the statistic does not show how many owners manage social media themselves, experience caption-writing difficulty, or would adopt or pay for Postra. [DTI, 2024 Philippine MSME Statistics](https://www.dti.gov.ph/dti-knowledge-hub/dti-statistics)

## 4. What competitors or alternatives already exist?

Interviewees reported using ChatGPT, Pinterest, or their own writing process. These are current alternatives: ChatGPT can draft copy, Pinterest can provide inspiration, and writing from scratch costs no additional software. Canva offers an AI Instagram caption generator, while Buffer's AI Assistant generates social captions and variations inside its publishing tools. [Canva AI Caption Generator](https://www.canva.com/ai-caption-generator/) · [Buffer AI Assistant](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-buffers-ai-assistant-qbBulfw9sn)

The existence of these tools means Postra cannot rely on “AI-generated captions” alone as its differentiator.

# Idea Validation Report (continued)

## 5. What is Postra's edge?

Postra uses a guided form rather than requiring a detailed open-ended prompt. It returns a caption, five hashtags, and a creative suggestion. All three participants said the captions were pretty accurate and used the app without asking for help. One participant found the creative suggestion interesting and potentially useful without a marketing background. In the follow-up, participants cited being busy, finding AI prompting challenging, and finding Postra easy and relevant. One participant wanted more control over audience targeting and narrative angle.

The working differentiation hypothesis is that Postra reduces prompt-writing effort and also gives a simple creative direction to business owners who lack marketing support. The feedback is promising but does not establish uniqueness or superiority over existing products.

## 6. What is a low-cost validation test?

I conducted three informal interviews and gave the participants the live app link to try independently. None needed help figuring out what to do. All three described the generated captions as pretty accurate. Two clearly said Postra could help; the third was open to trying it. One participant entered a website URL in the description field, which is useful to note when reviewing how users interpret the form. The interviewees were recruited through personal and work contacts, so this is a small convenience sample.

## 7. What are the biggest failure risks?

- Existing alternatives may be good enough, so users may not choose or return to Postra.
- Three interviews do not establish broad demand, repeat use, or willingness to pay.
- Users may need more control over target audience and narrative angle than the current form offers.
- Generated copy should stay grounded in the business details provided, especially when a user enters a short description or only a website address.
- The current feedback comes from a small, connected group and may not represent other Philippine MSMEs or industries.

## Conclusion

The interviews support a real early signal: these three business owners found Postra easy to use and its captions reasonably accurate. They also suggest that reducing prompt effort and offering creative direction may help owners without marketing support. The next validation should include more businesses, check whether they return to Postra for subsequent posts, and ask whether they would pay for it.
